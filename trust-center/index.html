Questions for your security review

What happens when a check cannot complete?

Inspection and authorization failures must keep protected content from falling back to a raw request. A Control Plane outage is a separate case. Verify the enforced local or last-known-good policy on your selected configuration.

What should we review for privileged access?

The server checks permissions for each operation. CAB signing requires CAB_DECISION_SIGN or Product Owner. Technical-file access requires TECHNICAL_DOSSIER_EXPORT, ASSET_VIEW or Product Owner, and the server checks that the system belongs to the current tenant. Audit read and export are checked separately.

What should we log?

Use scoped operational metadata and authorized audit records. Keep raw prompts, replies, pending operations, secrets and vault mappings out of telemetry and general application logs. Review retention separately for your application and configured provider.

What changes for self-hosted deployment?

The Control Plane location and operational responsibility change. The SDK still runs inside the Java process. Confirm the endpoint, policy distribution, identity, backup and recovery configuration for your installation.
