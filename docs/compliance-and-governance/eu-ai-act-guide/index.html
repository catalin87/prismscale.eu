EU AI Act Compliance Engine

The EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689) establishes the world's first comprehensive legal framework for artificial intelligence. Non-compliance carries fines of up to €35,000,000 or 7% of total worldwide annual turnover.

PrismScale provides a deterministic, legally validated compliance engine that classifies AI systems and guides organizations through mandatory obligations.

Risk Tier Classification Matrix​

The EU AI Act classifies AI systems into four distinct risk categories:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. UNACCEPTABLE RISK (Article 5)                                            │
│    Cognitive manipulation, social scoring, biometric categorization, etc.   │
│    👉 STRICTLY PROHIBITED IN THE EU                                         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. HIGH RISK (Article 6(1) & Article 6(2) + Annex III)                      │
│    Critical infrastructure, credit scoring, HR/recruitment, education, etc. │
│    👉 MANDATORY: Annex IV Technical File, CE Marking, Continuous Monitoring │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. TRANSPARENCY OBLIGATIONS (Article 50)                                    │
│    Customer-facing chatbots, synthetic media, deepfakes                     │
│    👉 MANDATORY: User disclosure ("You are interacting with an AI")         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. MINIMAL / LOW RISK (All other systems)                                   │
│    Spam filters, inventory forecasting, code autocompletion                 │
│    👉 Voluntarily follow codes of conduct                                   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Prohibited Practices Checklist (Article 5)​

The PrismScale engine checks for 8 statutory prohibited practices (PR-01 to PR-08):

  • PR-01: Subliminal techniques beyond human consciousness to distort behavior.
  • PR-02: Exploitation of vulnerabilities of specific groups (age, disability, socio-economic).
  • PR-03: Social scoring based on social behavior or personality traits.
  • PR-04: Individual risk assessments predicting criminal offenses based solely on profiling.
  • PR-05: Untargeted scraping of facial images from the internet or CCTV.
  • PR-06: Inferring emotions in workplace or educational institutions (with medical exceptions).
  • PR-07: Biometric categorization deducing race, political opinions, union membership, or religion.
  • PR-08: Real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces for law enforcement.

Rule: If any PR-* condition is TRUE, the system is immediately flagged as UNACCEPTABLE_RISK. Promotion to production is blocked.

High-Risk Use Cases (Annex III)​

Systems operating in the following domains are classified as HIGH_RISK under Article 6(2):

  • HR-AX-01: Biometrics (remote identification, emotion recognition).
  • HR-AX-02: Critical Infrastructure (safety components in electricity, water, gas, traffic).
  • HR-AX-03: Education & Vocational Training (admissions, evaluation, test scoring).
  • HR-AX-04: Employment & Worker Management (recruitment, performance evaluation, termination).
  • HR-AX-05: Access to Essential Private & Public Services (credit scoring, loan eligibility, health insurance risk).
  • HR-AX-06: Law Enforcement (polygraphs, evidence evaluation).
  • HR-AX-07: Migration, Asylum & Border Control.

The Hard Profiling Barrier (Article 6(3))​

Article 6(3) introduces a derogation allowing an Annex III system to be classified as non-high-risk if it performs only narrow procedural tasks or preparatory analysis.

The Absolute Barrier:
Article 6(3) explicitly states that if an AI system performs profiling of natural persons, the derogation is strictly disqualified. The system remains HIGH_RISK under all circumstances. PrismScale automatically enforces this rule in its decision tree.
