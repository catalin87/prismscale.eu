Annex IV Technical Documentation Dossier

Under Article 11 and Annex IV of the EU AI Act, providers of High-Risk AI systems must draw up and maintain comprehensive Technical Documentation before placing their system on the market or putting it into service.

PrismScale automates the compilation and export of audit-ready Annex IV dossiers in Markdown and print-ready PDF formats.

The 6 Mandatory Chapters​

Every dossier generated by PrismScale adheres strictly to the statutory structure defined in Annex IV:

Chapter 1: General Description of the AI System​

  • Legal entity, intended purpose, software versions, and hardware requirements.
  • Integration dependencies, upstream foundation models (e.g., GPT-4o, Claude 3.5), and API dependencies.

Chapter 2: Detailed Technical Specifications​

  • System architecture, algorithmic logic, and decision thresholds.
  • Input data requirements, preprocessing pipelines, and output formats.
  • Integration points with host microservices.

Chapter 3: Data Governance & Training Data​

  • Training, validation, and testing dataset provenance.
  • Bias mitigation strategies, demographic parity audits, and data labeling methodologies.
  • In-process PII masking safeguards (Zero Payload Egress).

Chapter 4: Risk Management System (Article 9)​

  • Continuous risk management matrix: identified risks, likelihood, severity, and mitigation controls.
  • Post-market monitoring triggers and failure analysis.

Chapter 5: Human Oversight Measures (Article 14)​

  • "Human-in-the-loop" (HITL) and "Human-on-the-loop" (HOTL) interfaces.
  • Change Advisory Board (CAB) approval trail and emergency stop mechanisms ("kill switches").

Chapter 6: Cybersecurity & Resilience (Article 15)​

  • In-process AI Firewall architecture (10 defense tiers).
  • Resistance against adversarial prompt injections, data poisoning, and model evasion.
  • Audit logging verification via Merkle tree chains.

Exporting Your Technical File​

In the PrismScale Web Console:

  1. Navigate to AI Systems Inventory -> Select your AI System.
  2. Click Compliance & Assessments -> Review the latest assessment results.
  3. Click Generate Annex IV Technical Dossier.
  4. Download the compiled dossier as an audit-ready PDF or Markdown archive to submit to your Notified Body or regulatory auditor.
